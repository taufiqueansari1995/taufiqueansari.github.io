About Me

I am a Centralized Document Controller with over 10 years of experience in the construction industry across the UAE and Saudi Arabia. I specialize in document control, EDMS administration, and project administration, ensuring accurate, compliant, and efficient management of engineering and project documentation across multiple projects. I have extensive experience using Aconex, Procore, SharePoint, and Microsoft Office to manage document control processes, including transmittals, document registers, revisions, approvals, project closeout documentation, and electronic archiving. I collaborate closely with project teams, engineering, QA/QC, procurement, HR, GR, and Consultant, client representatives to support efficient project execution and seamless information flow. Known for my attention to detail, organizational skills, and commitment to continuous improvement, I strive to deliver reliable document control and administrative support that contributes to successful project delivery..
